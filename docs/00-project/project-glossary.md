# Baghlaf Steel — Project Glossary

> The shared vocabulary for the Baghlaf Steel website project.

This document defines the preferred terminology used across the project's:

- Business documentation
- Strategy
- Content
- UX
- Figma designs
- Frontend code
- AI agent instructions
- QA documentation
- Client communications

The purpose is to maintain **terminology consistency, business accuracy, and clear communication** throughout the project.

---

# 1. How to Use This Glossary

The glossary has three purposes:

### 1.1 Shared Language

Everyone working on the project should use the same terminology for the same concept.

### 1.2 Business Accuracy

Terms must reflect Baghlaf Steel's actual business model.

### 1.3 AI Consistency

AI agents must use the definitions in this document instead of making assumptions from general industry terminology.

When a term is ambiguous, the agent must not guess.

It should refer to the relevant business documentation or flag the issue for human review.

---

# 2. Terminology Rules

## 2.1 Preferred Terminology

Use the terminology defined in this document wherever it accurately describes the subject.

## 2.2 Controlled Terminology

Some terms have specific meanings and must not be substituted casually.

Examples:

- Manufacturer ≠ Supplier
- Supply Source ≠ Partner
- Trader ≠ Manufacturer
- Product Capability ≠ Current Availability
- Requirement ≠ Quotation
- Verification ≠ Certification

## 2.3 Business Truth Takes Priority

If a general industry definition conflicts with an approved Baghlaf business definition, the approved Baghlaf definition takes precedence for this project.

## 2.4 Unknown Terms

If a required term is not defined here and its meaning could affect:

- Business representation
- Legal meaning
- Commercial meaning
- Technical accuracy
- Public content

the AI or developer must flag it for clarification instead of inventing a definition.

# 4. Business Model Terms

## Steel Trading

**Definition:**  
The commercial activity of buying, selling, sourcing, coordinating, or facilitating steel transactions without implying that the company itself manufactures the material.

**Baghlaf context:**  
Baghlaf Steel operates within steel trading.

---

## Steel Supply

**Definition:**  
The provision or coordination of steel material to meet a buyer's requirements.

**Baghlaf context:**  
Steel supply is a core part of Baghlaf Steel's business positioning.

---

## Steel Trading & Supply

**Definition:**  
The preferred high-level description of Baghlaf Steel's business activity.

**Preferred usage:**  
Use this phrase when describing the company's overall commercial role.

---

## Trading Company

**Definition:**  
A company whose role includes commercial sourcing, buying, selling, and/or coordinating products rather than necessarily manufacturing them.

**Baghlaf context:**  
Baghlaf Steel is a trading company.

---

## Supply Coordination

**Definition:**  
The process of coordinating the commercial and operational steps required to connect a buyer's requirement with an appropriate supply source.

---

## Sourcing

**Definition:**  
The process of identifying and obtaining suitable material from an appropriate supply source according to a buyer's requirements.

---

## Requirement-Based Sourcing

**Definition:**  
A sourcing approach in which product selection and supply coordination are driven by the buyer's actual technical, quantitative, commercial, and delivery requirements.

# 5. Steel Industry Relationship Terms

These terms are **controlled terminology** and must not be used interchangeably.

---

## Manufacturer

**Definition:**  
An organization that physically manufactures or produces the steel material.

**Baghlaf rule:**  
Baghlaf Steel is not to be presented as a manufacturer unless management explicitly confirms otherwise.

---

## Supply Source

**Definition:**  
The organization or source from which the required steel material may be obtained.

A supply source may be a manufacturer or another approved source depending on the specific transaction.

**Preferred usage:**  
Use "supply source" when the exact commercial relationship does not need to be stated publicly or has not yet been approved at a more specific level.

---

## Supplier

**Definition:**  
An organization that supplies material to Baghlaf Steel or directly into a transaction according to an established commercial arrangement.

**Important rule:**  
Do not use "supplier" merely because an organization manufactures steel.

The relationship must be confirmed.

---

## Partner

**Definition:**  
An organization with a formally established business relationship that Baghlaf Steel is authorized to describe publicly as a partner.

**Important rule:**  
Do not use "partner" as a marketing synonym for supplier, manufacturer, or supply source.

---

## Authorized Distributor

**Definition:**  
A company that has formal authorization from a manufacturer or relevant brand to distribute its products.

**Important rule:**  
This term requires explicit evidence and management approval before public use.

---

## Exclusive Partner

**Definition:**  
A partner with formally defined exclusive rights or arrangements.

**Important rule:**  
This is a high-risk commercial claim and must never be used without explicit documentary evidence and management approval.

---

## Commercial Relationship

**Definition:**  
A business relationship between Baghlaf Steel and another organization that may involve sourcing, purchasing, selling, coordination, or another agreed commercial activity.

This term is intentionally broad and should be replaced by a more precise relationship type whenever verified information exists.

---

## Manufacturer Relationship

**Definition:**  
The specific relationship between Baghlaf Steel and a manufacturer or manufacturing organization.

The exact relationship must be classified accurately before public representation.

---

# 6. Product Terms

## Product

**Definition:**  
A specific steel material or steel product that Baghlaf Steel is approved to present as part of its supply offering.

**Important rule:**  
A product must be supported by approved business/product information before being published.

---

## Product Category

**Definition:**  
A logical grouping of related steel products.

Examples may include long products, flat products, or structural products, but the final taxonomy must be based on Baghlaf's actual portfolio.

---

## Product Capability

**Definition:**  
The ability of Baghlaf Steel to source and/or supply a particular product under appropriate circumstances.

**Important distinction:**  
Product capability does not mean that the product is currently in stock or immediately available.

---

## Current Availability

**Definition:**  
The confirmed availability of a specific product, quantity, specification, or allocation at a particular time.

**Important rule:**  
Do not infer current availability from a public product page.

---

## Specification

**Definition:**  
The technical requirements that define the requested or supplied material.

Specifications may include:

- Grade
- Standard
- Dimensions
- Thickness
- Diameter
- Form
- Other technical characteristics

---

## Grade

**Definition:**  
The defined material grade or classification applicable to a steel product.

---

## Standard

**Definition:**  
A recognized technical standard or specification system applicable to the product.

Examples may include recognized international or national standards.

Only standards confirmed for a product may be published.

---

## Product Form

**Definition:**  
The physical form in which a product is supplied.

Examples may include:

- Bar
- Coil
- Sheet
- Plate
- Wire
- Section

The actual forms available depend on the approved product data.

---

## Technical Data

**Definition:**  
Structured technical information describing a product.

This may include specifications, dimensions, grades, standards, properties, and other relevant information.

---

## Product Document

**Definition:**  
An approved document associated with a product.

Examples may include:

- Technical datasheet
- Specification sheet
- Certificate
- Approved product documentation

# 18. Capitalization & Abbreviation Rules

Use standard capitalization for formal terms.

### First use

Write the full term followed by the abbreviation:

> Letter of Credit (LC)

> Request for Quotation (RFQ)

> Right-to-Left (RTL)

### Later use

Use the abbreviation where appropriate:

> LC

> RFQ

> RTL

Avoid introducing abbreviations when they make the content harder for a general international buyer to understand.

# 19. AI Terminology Rules

AI agents must:

1. Use this glossary as the project's shared vocabulary.
2. Preserve controlled terminology.
3. Never substitute "partner" for "supplier" without evidence.
4. Never substitute "manufacturer" for "supply source."
5. Never describe Baghlaf Steel as a manufacturer without explicit approval.
6. Never treat product capability as current availability.
7. Never treat an inquiry as an order unless an order has actually been placed.
8. Never invent definitions for ambiguous business terms.
9. Flag terminology conflicts rather than resolving them through assumption.
10. Preserve approved terminology even when stylistic alternatives may appear more natural.
