# Strategic Framework

Parts 1–18 constitute the strategic-to-production framework for the Baghlaf Steel website.

The framework defines the project's direction from:

**Strategy → Business Model → Buyer Experience → Design → Architecture → AI-Assisted Development → QA → Production → Launch & Maintenance**

Start with [`../00-project/strategic-framework-index.md`](../00-project/strategic-framework-index.md) for the master map, document sequence, and navigation guide.

## Parts

1. Company Identity
2. Business Model
3. Target Buyers
4. Positioning
5. Trust Architecture
6. Buyer Journey & Conversion
7. Information Architecture & Sitemap
8. Content Strategy & Messaging
9. Product & Supply Network
10. Brand & Visual Direction
11. Figma Design System
12. UX Wireframes & Page Experience
13. Technical Architecture
14. AI Agent Architecture & Project Memory
15. Development Workflow & Delivery
16. QA, Testing & Visual Validation
17. Deployment, Security & Production
18. Launch, Handoff & Maintenance

## Important

This framework defines **strategy, direction, architecture, and operating principles**.

It does **not** replace the management-approved business source of truth.

Approved business facts are maintained separately under:

[`../02-business/`](../02-business/)

When strategy, documentation, implementation, or AI-generated suggestions conflict with approved business facts, the approved business information takes precedence within the business domain.
