# Baghlaf Steel — Company Identity

> Strategic definition of Baghlaf Steel's identity, role, positioning context, and public representation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic identity of Baghlaf Steel for the website project.

It establishes how the company should be understood and represented across:

- Website content
- Brand communication
- UX
- Visual design
- Product presentation
- Trust and verification
- AI-assisted development

This document defines strategic direction.

It does not replace management-approved business facts stored in:

`docs/02-business/`

---

## Public Brand

**Baghlaf Steel**

### Status

Working identity.

### Authority

The exact official public brand name should be confirmed by Baghlaf management and recorded in:

`docs/02-business/company-facts.md`

---

# 3. Geographic Identity

Baghlaf Steel is positioned as a Saudi-based business serving international markets.

## Country

**Saudi Arabia**

## Market Orientation

**International B2B**

The website should communicate Saudi origin as part of the company's identity while presenting the company to an international professional audience.

---

# 4. Business Identity

The current strategic representation is:

> **Baghlaf Steel is a steel trading and supply company serving international B2B buyers.**

Its role is centered on:

- Steel trading
- Steel sourcing
- Steel supply
- Requirement-based sourcing
- Commercial coordination
- Connecting buyer requirements with suitable supply sources

### Critical distinction

Baghlaf Steel should **not** be represented as a steel manufacturer unless management explicitly confirms and approves such a change.

---

# 5. What Baghlaf Steel Is

For the purposes of this project, Baghlaf Steel is positioned as:

### A Saudi steel trading and supply company

Its value lies in helping buyers identify and coordinate suitable steel supply according to:

- Product requirements
- Technical specifications
- Quantity
- Commercial requirements
- Destination
- Delivery requirements

The website should make this role easy to understand.

---

# 6. What Baghlaf Steel Is Not

The website must not present Baghlaf Steel as:

- A steel manufacturing company
- A steel mill
- An owner/operator of manufacturing facilities
- A producer of steel products
- A manufacturer merely because it sources material from manufacturers

unless such statements are explicitly verified and approved by management.

---

# 7. Relationship With External Manufacturers

Baghlaf Steel may source material from major steel manufacturers or other approved supply sources in Saudi Arabia.

The existence of a sourcing relationship does not automatically establish that Baghlaf Steel is:

- The manufacturer
- The owner of the facility
- A partner
- An authorized distributor
- An exclusive distributor
- An agent

Each relationship must be classified according to verified business information.

See:

`docs/02-business/approved-supply-sources.md`

for authoritative relationship information.

---

# 9. Value of the Identity

The company's value should not depend on claiming ownership of manufacturing assets.

Instead, the website should communicate value through:

### Access

Access to suitable steel supply sources.

### Coordination

Commercial coordination between buyer requirements and supply.

### Understanding

Understanding of buyer specifications and commercial needs.

### Responsiveness

A professional path for submitting and handling requirements.

### Transparency

Clear information about Baghlaf's role and supply model.

# 10. Identity & Trust

Because international buyers may encounter multiple websites, brokers, intermediaries, or individuals claiming to represent Baghlaf Steel, company identity is also a trust problem.

The website should therefore make the official identity easy to verify.

      ↓

# 12. Corporate Identity vs Family / Related Entities

Baghlaf Steel may have relationships with other Baghlaf-related entities.

The website must distinguish Baghlaf Steel from any other company or entity unless management provides approved wording describing a relationship.

The project must not independently infer or publish:

- Ownership relationships
- Corporate group membership
- Historical ownership claims
- Legal relationships
- Separation arrangements
- Family relationships

These are management-controlled business facts.

---

# 13. Public Identity Principles

The website should present Baghlaf Steel as:

### Clear

A visitor should understand what the company does quickly.

### Professional

The company should appear credible to international B2B buyers.

### Precise

Business terminology should reflect the company's actual role.

### Transparent

The website should explain the supply model without creating misleading impressions.

### Verifiable

Official identity and communication channels should be easy to confirm.

### International

The presentation should meet the expectations of professional global buyers.

### Saudi

Saudi origin should be authentically represented without relying on superficial national symbolism.

---

Preferred language should emphasize:

```
Trading
Supply
Sourcing
Coordination
Requirements
Products
Saudi Supply
International Buyers
Verification
```

Avoid language that implies manufacturing unless verified.

Examples of risky language include:

```
Our Factory
Our Mill
Our Production
Our Manufacturing Facilities
Our Production Capacity
```

These terms require explicit factual support before use.

---

# 15. Identity Tone

The company should communicate with a tone that is:

- Confident
- Professional
- Precise
- Direct
- Commercial
- Trustworthy
- International

Avoid:

- Exaggerated marketing claims
- Aggressive sales language
- Unsupported superlatives
- Artificial corporate language
- Claims that cannot be verified

Preferred principle:

> **Proof over slogans.**

---

# 16. Identity for International Buyers

The website should assume that many international visitors may not know Baghlaf Steel beforehand.

Therefore, the identity should answer quickly:

```
Who are you?
      ↓
What do you do?
      ↓
Where are you based?
      ↓
How do you supply?
      ↓
Can I verify you?
      ↓
Can I submit my requirement?
```

The homepage and navigation should support this progression.

# 23. AI Rules for Company Identity

AI agents must:

1. Use the approved Baghlaf identity consistently.
2. Never invent legal or ownership information.
3. Never describe Baghlaf as a manufacturer without explicit approval.
4. Never imply ownership of external manufacturing facilities.
5. Never infer corporate relationships.
6. Use approved company terminology.
7. Refer to `docs/02-business/` for factual company information.
8. Flag contradictions rather than choosing an assumption.
9. Preserve the distinction between Baghlaf and external supply sources.
10. Treat missing information as unknown or pending verification.

---

# 24. Identity Success Criteria

The website successfully communicates Baghlaf Steel's identity when a new international visitor can understand:

```
✓ Baghlaf is a Saudi company.

✓ Baghlaf operates in steel trading and supply.

✓ Baghlaf is not being presented as the steel manufacturer.

✓ Baghlaf connects buyer requirements with suitable
  steel supply sources.

✓ Products represent supply capability.

✓ Official identity can be independently verified.

✓ A legitimate buyer knows how to begin a commercial inquiry.
```
