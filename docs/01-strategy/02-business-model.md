# Baghlaf Steel — Business Model

> Strategic definition of how Baghlaf Steel creates value, interacts with buyers and supply sources, and participates in international steel transactions.

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic business model that the Baghlaf Steel website should communicate.

It explains:

- What Baghlaf Steel does
- What role Baghlaf plays in the steel value chain
- How buyer requirements are handled
- How steel is sourced
- How Baghlaf creates commercial value
- How external manufacturers and supply sources relate to Baghlaf
- What the website should and should not imply about the business model

This document defines strategic direction.

It does not replace management-approved business facts stored in:

`docs/02-business/`

---

# 2. Business Model at a Glance

The current strategic understanding is:

> **Baghlaf Steel is a Saudi steel trading and supply company serving international B2B buyers through requirement-based sourcing and commercial coordination.**

The conceptual model is:

```text
International Buyer
        ↓
Steel Requirement
        ↓
Baghlaf Steel
        ↓
Requirement Assessment
        ↓
Sourcing / Supply Coordination
        ↓
Suitable Supply Source
        ↓
Commercial Coordination
        ↓
Supply
        ↓
International Buyer
```

The exact operational sequence must be confirmed against Baghlaf's actual commercial process.

---

# 3. Core Business Identity

Baghlaf Steel should be represented as a:

# Steel Trading & Supply Company

Its commercial role is based on:

- Trading steel
- Sourcing suitable steel
- Coordinating supply
- Understanding buyer requirements
- Connecting requirements with appropriate supply sources
- Facilitating commercial transactions

Baghlaf's value does not depend on representing itself as the manufacturer of the steel.

---

# 4. What Baghlaf Does

At a strategic level, Baghlaf Steel may:

### Receive Buyer Requirements

Understand what the buyer requires in terms of:

- Product
- Quantity
- Grade
- Standard
- Dimensions
- Destination
- Delivery requirements
- Commercial requirements

### Assess Requirements

Evaluate whether the requirement can potentially be addressed through an appropriate supply route.

### Source Material

Identify suitable steel supply from relevant and approved supply sources.

### Coordinate Commercially

Coordinate the relevant commercial communication and transaction requirements between the buyer and applicable supply sources.

### Supply Steel

Facilitate the provision of the agreed material according to the terms of the transaction.

The exact responsibilities at each stage require management confirmation.

---

# 5. What Baghlaf Does Not Represent

Baghlaf Steel must not be represented as:

- A steel mill
- A steel manufacturer
- The producer of steel sourced from external manufacturers
- The owner of external manufacturing facilities
- The operator of external manufacturing facilities

unless this is explicitly verified and approved by management.

# 7. Buyer-Centered Business Model

The website should explain the business from the buyer's perspective.

The buyer begins with a requirement:

```
"I need this steel."
```

Baghlaf's role is to help transform that requirement into an appropriate supply opportunity.

Conceptually:

```
Buyer Need
    ↓
Specification
    ↓
Assessment
    ↓
Sourcing
    ↓
Commercial Coordination
    ↓
Supply
```

This should form the foundation of the website's commercial storytelling.

---

# 8. Requirement-Based Sourcing

A core strategic principle is:

> **Supply should be driven by the buyer's actual requirement rather than by a generic product catalog alone.**

A requirement may include:

- Product type
- Quantity
- Grade
- Standard
- Dimensions
- Form
- Destination
- Delivery timing
- Documentation
- Commercial conditions

The actual fields required for a valid Baghlaf requirement must be confirmed by management.

---

# 9. Product Capability vs Current Availability

The website must distinguish between:

### Product Capability

A product that Baghlaf is able to source or potentially supply.

### Current Availability

A product, quantity, or specification that has been specifically confirmed as available at a particular time.

These concepts must not be treated as equivalent.

A public product page should not automatically imply:

> "This material is currently in stock."

unless such availability is explicitly confirmed.

---

# 10. Supply Sources

Baghlaf Steel may source material from external steel manufacturers and other approved supply sources.

A supply source may represent:

- A manufacturer
- A supplier
- Another approved source

depending on the verified commercial relationship.

The exact relationship must be documented and approved before being publicly represented.

See:

`docs/02-business/approved-supply-sources.md`

# 11. Manufacturer vs Supply Source

This distinction is fundamental to the Baghlaf business model.

```
Manufacturer
    ↓
Physically produces steel

Supply Source
    ↓
Source from which Baghlaf may obtain steel

Baghlaf Steel
    ↓
Trades / sources / coordinates supply

Buyer
    ↓
Purchases or receives the required steel
```

The terms must not be used interchangeably.

---

# 12. External Manufacturer Relationships

An external manufacturer's relationship with Baghlaf Steel must be described according to the actual verified relationship.

Possible relationship classifications may include:

- Supply Source
- Supplier
- Commercial Partner
- Partner
- Distributor
- Authorized Distributor
- Agent
- Other approved relationship

These categories are controlled terminology.

The presence of a sourcing relationship does not automatically establish any of them.

# 13. Commercial Value Proposition

The business model should communicate value through:

## Access

Access to appropriate steel supply sources.

## Sourcing

The ability to identify potential supply routes based on buyer requirements.

## Coordination

Coordination of commercial and operational requirements.

## Responsiveness

A clear route for serious buyers to communicate their requirements.

## Transparency

Clear representation of Baghlaf's actual role in the transaction.

## International Orientation

A business experience designed for professional B2B buyers.

# 14. Role of the Product Catalog

The product catalog serves as a way to communicate:

> **What Baghlaf Steel can potentially source or supply.**

It is not necessarily a live inventory system.

The website should therefore avoid creating the impression of real-time stock unless a verified system exists to support such information.

---

# 15. Role of the Supply Network

The Supply Network section exists to explain:

> **How Baghlaf connects buyer requirements with suitable sources of steel supply.**

Its purpose is not to create the impression that Baghlaf owns the entire upstream manufacturing network.

A conceptual model is:

```
                    BAGHLAF STEEL
                          │
                 Buyer Requirement
                          │
          ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
          ↓               ↓               ↓
     Supply Source A  Supply Source B  Supply Source C
          │               │               │
          └───────────────┼───────────────┘
                          ↓
                    Buyer / Delivery
```

The actual published sources and relationships must be approved by management.

- Another commercial condition

Until confirmed:

# 25. Business Model and Trust

The trading-and-supply model should strengthen rather than weaken trust.

Transparency should answer:

```
Who is Baghlaf?
       ↓
What role does Baghlaf play?
       ↓
Where can suitable steel come from?
       ↓
How is a buyer requirement handled?
       ↓
Who can the buyer officially contact?
```

This is preferable to using vague language such as:

> "We manufacture and supply premium steel."

when the manufacturing part is not accurate.

# 37. Final Strategic Principle

> **Baghlaf Steel creates value through steel trading, requirement-based sourcing, supply coordination, and international commercial engagement—not by presenting itself as the manufacturer of externally produced steel. The website must make this role clear, credible, and transparent while preserving precise distinctions between products, manufacturers, supply sources, suppliers, and other commercial relationships.**

```

### Why I structured it this way

There are a few particularly important decisions in this version.

**First:** I did not state that Hadeed, Al-Rajhi Steel, Al-Ittefaq, or Al-Watania are approved Baghlaf supply relationships inside this strategy document. Those names belong in `approved-supply-sources.md` only after management confirms the exact relationship.

**Second:** I explicitly isolated the `100,000 MT` issue. That number is important because it appeared in our earlier discussion, but it must **not accidentally become an official MOQ simply because it appears in project documentation**.

**Third:** I kept the actual commercial workflow as a **conceptual model** until management explains the real process. That gives us a safe foundation without inventing how Baghlaf operates.

### One structural improvement I recommend

For this file, don't let Antigravity "improve" the language by turning phrases such as:

> "may source"

into:

> "sources"

or:

> "may potentially supply"

into:

> "supplies"

Those small grammatical changes can convert a **capability statement into a factual business claim**.

For this project, that distinction matters a lot.

Once management provides the answers to the business questionnaire, we'll be able to revise this document from **working strategic model → management-confirmed business model** without having to rebuild its structure.
```
