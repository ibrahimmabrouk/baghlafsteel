# 8.1 Strategic Objective

The content strategy exists to ensure that every word on the Baghlaf Steel website serves one of three purposes:

```text
1. Explain
2. Prove
3. Convert
```

The website should not be filled with generic corporate copy.

Every important piece of content should help the buyer:

> **Understand what Baghlaf Steel is → understand what it can do → verify that it is legitimate → decide whether to engage.**

The central content principle is:

# **Clarity Before Persuasion**

Baghlaf does not need to convince buyers through exaggerated language.

It needs to make the business understandable and provide credible evidence.

---

# 8.2 Content Philosophy

The website should follow five core principles.

## 1. Say Exactly What the Company Is

Baghlaf Steel should consistently be described as:

> **A Saudi steel trading and supply company.**

Never imply that the company is a manufacturer unless management later provides verified information that changes this position.

---

## 2. Explain the Business Model Positively

Being a trading and supply company should not sound like a limitation.

The content should explain the value of:

- Sourcing
    
- Supply access
    
- Requirement assessment
    
- Commercial coordination
    
- International trade
    
- Large-volume order coordination
    

The message is:

> **Baghlaf's value is in connecting demand with suitable Saudi steel supply.**

---

## 3. Replace Marketing Claims With Evidence

Instead of:

> "We are a world-class steel company."

Prefer:

> "Baghlaf Steel coordinates steel supply from Saudi Arabia according to buyer specifications, quantities, and commercial requirements."

The second communicates an actual capability.

---

## 4. Write for Experienced B2B Buyers

The primary reader should be treated as a professional.

Avoid explaining obvious concepts excessively.

Avoid sales language designed for consumers.

Use precise commercial and technical language.

---

## 5. Never Let AI Fill Information Gaps

When information is missing:

> **Flag it. Do not invent it.**

This is a critical content rule for the future AI development workflow.

# 8.13 Product Copywriting Principle

Product pages should be:

**technical where necessary, concise where possible.**

Avoid descriptions such as:

> "Our premium high-quality steel products are designed to meet all your industrial needs."

> "Available in [grade], according to [standard], subject to the buyer's required specification and supply availability."

The second is more useful to a procurement professional.
