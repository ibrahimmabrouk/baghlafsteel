# Part 1 - Company Identity

## 1.1 Company Overview

**Baghlaf Steel** is a Saudi-based steel trading and supply company serving buyers who require steel products in substantial quantities and according to defined technical, commercial, and delivery requirements.

Baghlaf Steel is **not presented as a steel manufacturer**. Instead, the company operates as a commercial intermediary and supply coordinator, sourcing steel from major manufacturers in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia according to the requirements of each transaction.

### Core distinction

> **Baghlaf Steel supplies steel, it does not manufacture the steel itself.**

This distinction is foundational to the company's website, brand messaging, and all future AI-generated content.

---

## 1.2 Business Identity

### Brand Name

**Baghlaf Steel**

### Primary Business Category

**Steel Trading & Supply**

### Geographic Identity

**Saudi Arabia**

### Market Orientation

**International B2B**

### Primary Commercial Role

Baghlaf Steel acts as a bridge between international steel buyers and suitable steel supply sources within Saudi Arabia.

---

## 1.3 Corporate Relationship

Management has indicated that Baghlaf Steel belongs to the Baghlaf family but operates separately from Baghlaf Holding Group following the death of the family's majority owner.

### Strategic rule

The website must **not publish a detailed description of the relationship, separation, ownership history, or legal status of the relationship with Baghlaf Holding Group until management provides approved wording and supporting information.**

For now, the website strategy should simply establish:

> **Baghlaf Steel is the company represented by the official website at `baghlaf.com.sa`.**

Any future statement concerning its relationship with other Baghlaf entities should be explicitly approved by management.

---

## 1.4 Digital Identity

Because the exact `baghlafsteel.com` domain is understood to be owned by another Baghlaf-related entity, the website should establish a separate, authoritative digital identity.

### Proposed canonical identity

**Official Website:** `baghlaf.com.sa`

The website should consistently connect:

```text
Baghlaf Steel
      ↓
baghlaf.com.sa
      ↓
@baghlaf.com.sa email addresses
      ↓
Official phone numbers
      ↓
Official company information
      ↓
Official social profiles
```

The purpose is to make the domain itself part of the company's verification infrastructure.

---

## 1.5 Identity Principles

The website must communicate:

**Clarity** — Visitors should immediately understand what Baghlaf Steel is.

**Authenticity** — The company should be distinguishable from unauthorized intermediaries and unrelated parties.

**Accuracy** — Every corporate claim must be factually supportable.

**Professionalism** — Communication should meet the expectations of international B2B procurement organizations.

**Transparency** — Baghlaf should clearly explain its role in the supply chain rather than implying that it is a manufacturer.

# Part 2 — Business Model

## 2.1 How Baghlaf Steel Operates

The core business model can be represented as:

```text
International Buyer
        │
        │
        │ Product
        │ Specification
        │ Quantity
        │ Destination
        │ Commercial Requirements
        ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐
│     BAGHLAF STEEL       │
│                         │
│ Trading & Supply        │
│ Coordination            │
└────────────┬────────────┘
             │
             │ Source / Coordinate
             ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Saudi Steel Supply Sources   │
│                              │
│ Major Manufacturers          │
│ + Other Qualified Sources    │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │
               ▼
        International Buyer
```

---

## 2.2 Buyer Requirement

A transaction begins with a buyer's requirement.

The requirement may include:

- Product type
    
- Steel grade
    
- Applicable standard
    
- Quantity
    
- Dimensions or specifications
    
- Destination
    
- Delivery requirements
    
- Inspection requirements
    
- Documentation requirements
    
- Payment/commercial terms
    
- Letter of Credit or other agreed payment mechanism
    

The website should therefore position Baghlaf as a company capable of handling **specific procurement requirements**, rather than merely selling generic steel products.

---

## 2.3 Sourcing

Once a requirement is received, Baghlaf assesses the requirement and identifies an appropriate supply route.

The company may source from major steel manufacturers in Saudi Arabia, including manufacturers previously mentioned by management such as:

- Hadeed
    
- Al-Rajhi Steel
    
- Al-Ittefaq
    
- Al-Watania
    
- Other suitable sources
    

### Important publication rule

The website must not automatically describe these companies as:

- Official partners
    
- Authorized distributors
    
- Exclusive suppliers
    
- Strategic partners
    
- Contractual partners
    

unless the relevant relationship has been verified and management has approved the wording.

For the initial website strategy, the safer concept is:

> **Baghlaf Steel sources steel through its Saudi supply network according to buyer requirements.**

---

## 2.4 High-Volume Orders

Management has indicated that transactions may involve very large quantities, with **100,000 metric tons mentioned as a minimum order example**.

This should currently be treated as **management-provided information requiring confirmation before public publication as a universal minimum order requirement**.

Until confirmed, the website should use broader language such as:

> **Large-volume steel requirements**

or:

> **Built to coordinate substantial international steel orders.**

The final commercial threshold should be defined by management.

---

## 2.5 Baghlaf's Value in the Supply Chain

Baghlaf's value is not primarily the ownership of manufacturing assets.

Its value lies in the ability to:

1. Understand the buyer's requirement.
    
2. Identify suitable supply sources.
    
3. Coordinate commercial and technical requirements.
    
4. Facilitate the transaction.
    
5. Coordinate supply and documentation.
    
6. Support the buyer through the agreed commercial process.
    

This gives Baghlaf a positioning opportunity around:

> **Access, coordination, sourcing, and execution.**

---

## 2.6 Business Model Statement

### Working definition

> **Baghlaf Steel is a Saudi steel trading and supply company that connects international buyers with suitable steel supply sources in the Kingdom, coordinating sourcing and commercial requirements according to the buyer's specifications, quantity, and delivery needs.**

This is the current strategic definition and should be reviewed by management before becoming final public copy.

# Part 3 — Target Buyers

## 3.1 Primary Market

Baghlaf Steel should be designed primarily for:

> **International B2B buyers seeking substantial volumes of steel from Saudi Arabia.**

The website should not feel like a consumer steel retailer or a local hardware supplier.

Its tone and information architecture should reflect:

**International procurement + industrial supply + commodity trading.**

---

# 3.2 Buyer Persona A — International Procurement Manager

### Situation

A procurement professional has a defined requirement and needs to identify a credible Saudi source.

### Typical questions

> Can you supply the required quantity?

> Can you meet the required specification?

> Where does the material come from?

> How does the transaction work?

> What documentation is available?

> Who is the actual company I am dealing with?

### Website needs

This buyer needs:

- Clear product information
    
- Specifications
    
- Supply process
    
- Commercial process
    
- Credibility signals
    
- Company verification
    
- Fast contact
    
- Requirement submission
    

---

# 3.3 Buyer Persona B — International Steel Trader / Commodity Buyer

### Situation

The buyer is commercially experienced and is evaluating potential supply channels for large transactions.

### Main concerns

- Reliability
    
- Quantity
    
- Pricing
    
- Supply availability
    
- Specifications
    
- Commercial terms
    
- Documentation
    
- Execution capability
    
- Communication
    

### Website needs

This buyer expects the website to feel:

**professional, direct, commercially literate, and credible.**

Excessive marketing language would reduce credibility.

---

# 3.4 Buyer Persona C — New / Unfamiliar Buyer

This persona is particularly important for Baghlaf.

The buyer may have discovered the company through:

- Email
    
- LinkedIn
    
- Broker
    
- Referral
    
- Trade network
    
- Search engine
    
- WhatsApp
    
- Industry contact
    

The buyer's first question may not be:

> "What products do you sell?"

It may be:

> **"Is this really Baghlaf Steel?"**

### Website needs

This buyer needs an obvious verification mechanism:

**Official Website**

**Official Email Domain**

**Official Phone Numbers**

**Company Information**

**Official Contacts**

**Verification Guidance**

This persona is one of the reasons trust should be treated as a core product requirement rather than merely a visual design objective.

---

# 3.5 Buyer Persona D — Existing / Referred Buyer

An existing contact may already know Baghlaf through direct business relationships.

This buyer primarily needs:

- Product information
    
- Commercial communication
    
- Contact details
    
- Submission of new requirements
    
- Documentation/resources
    
- Fast access to relevant departments
    

The website should therefore remain useful even after trust has already been established.

---

# 3.6 Buyer Journey

The likely buyer journey should be designed around these questions:

```text
Who are you?
      ↓
Are you legitimate?
      ↓
What exactly do you supply?
      ↓
Where is the material sourced?
      ↓
Can you handle my requirement?
      ↓
How does the process work?
      ↓
Can I trust the commercial process?
      ↓
How do I contact the official company?
      ↓
Submit Requirement
```

This sequence should influence both the homepage and the full website information architecture.

# Part 4 — Positioning

## 4.1 Positioning Challenge

Baghlaf Steel faces a specific market challenge:

**The company must establish credibility while avoiding a false perception that it is itself a steel manufacturer.**

At the same time, international buyers need confidence that they are dealing with the genuine Baghlaf Steel rather than an unauthorized broker or intermediary.

Therefore, Baghlaf's positioning needs to combine:

**Authenticity + Access + Supply Capability + Commercial Professionalism.**

---

# 4.2 Recommended Positioning

### Primary positioning

> **Saudi Steel Trading & Supply**

### Supporting positioning

> **Connecting international buyers with steel supply from Saudi Arabia.**

### Expanded positioning

> **Baghlaf Steel is a Saudi-based steel trading and supply company that sources and coordinates steel supply according to international buyers' specifications, quantities, and commercial requirements.**

These are strategic working statements, not yet final approved website copy.

---

# 4.3 Value Proposition

Baghlaf should communicate four central value propositions.

### 1. Access to Saudi Supply

Baghlaf provides international buyers with access to steel supply sources within the Kingdom.

### 2. Requirement-Based Sourcing

The company works around the buyer's actual product, specification, quantity, and delivery requirement.

### 3. Commercial Coordination

Baghlaf coordinates the commercial process between the buyer and the relevant supply source.

### 4. Direct & Verifiable Identity

Baghlaf provides an official digital identity through `baghlaf.com.sa` and clearly identifies official communication channels.

---

# 4.4 What Baghlaf Is

The website should portray Baghlaf as:

```text
A Saudi company
        +
A steel trading company
        +
A supply coordinator
        +
A connection to Saudi steel supply
        +
An international B2B commercial partner
```

---

# 4.5 What Baghlaf Is Not

The website must never implicitly or explicitly present Baghlaf as a manufacturer unless management later confirms that a specific manufacturing activity belongs to the company.

Avoid unsupported language such as:

> "Our factories"

> "Our steel mill"

> "Our production facilities"

> "Our manufacturing capacity"

> "We manufacture steel"

> "Our steel production"

unless each statement is factually verified.

---

# 4.6 Competitive Differentiation

The website should avoid competing primarily through generic claims such as:

> "High quality"

> "Best prices"

> "World-class service"

> "Leading company"

> "Unmatched quality"

Instead, differentiation should be demonstrated through specific evidence:

```text
Saudi sourcing network
        +
Requirement-based sourcing
        +
Large-volume capability
        +
Commercial coordination
        +
Transparent process
        +
Official verification
        +
Professional international communication
```

The goal is:

> **Proof over slogans.**

---

# 4.7 Brand Personality

The recommended brand personality is:

### Trustworthy

The company communicates carefully and avoids exaggerated claims.

### Industrial

The visual and verbal identity should reflect the steel and infrastructure sector.

### Precise

Specifications, quantities, standards, documents, and commercial requirements should be treated seriously.

### International

The experience should be appropriate for buyers outside Saudi Arabia.

### Confident

The company should communicate capabilities without unnecessary exaggeration.

### Saudi

The company's Saudi identity should be an asset rather than something hidden.

---

# 4.8 Recommended Strategic Message

A possible strategic message hierarchy is:

### Primary

**Steel Supply from Saudi Arabia.**

### Secondary

**Connecting international buyers with reliable Saudi steel supply.**

### Supporting

**Sourcing and coordinating steel according to your requirements.**

### Trust

**Deal directly with the official Baghlaf Steel team.**

These are conceptual directions and should be refined after management review.

---

# 4.9 Strategic Positioning Statement

### Internal version

> **Baghlaf Steel is a Saudi-based steel trading and supply company serving international B2B buyers by sourcing and coordinating steel supply through suitable Saudi manufacturers and supply sources according to buyer specifications, quantities, and commercial requirements. Its competitive position is based on access to Saudi supply, requirement-based sourcing, commercial coordination, and a transparent, verifiable corporate identity.**

---

# 4.10 Strategic North Star

The website should make a qualified international buyer leave with four clear conclusions:

```text
1. This is the real Baghlaf Steel.

2. Baghlaf Steel is a trading and supply company,
   not a manufacturer pretending to be one.

3. They can connect my requirement with
   suitable Saudi steel supply.

4. I know exactly how to contact and verify
   the official company.
```

If the website accomplishes these four things clearly, it is succeeding at its most important strategic objective.
