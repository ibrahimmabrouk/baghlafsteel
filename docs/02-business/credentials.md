# Credentials

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative business source of truth for Baghlaf Steel's verified credentials, registrations, licenses, certifications, memberships, accreditations, awards, and other formal evidence that may support the company's public or internal representation.

The purpose is to ensure that the website presents credentials accurately and does not:

- invent credentials;
- misattribute credentials;
- present expired credentials as current;
- present another company's credential as Baghlaf's;
- confuse a product certificate with a company certification;
- publish credential numbers without appropriate approval;
- imply broader scope than the credential actually provides.

Only credentials that have been appropriately verified and approved may be used as public evidence.

---

# 1. Credential Status

| Status | Meaning | Public Use |
|---|---|---|
| Verified | Credential has been verified against an appropriate source. | Depends on approval |
| Approved | Verified and explicitly approved for the specified public/internal use. | Yes, within scope |
| Pending Verification | Credential has been provided but authenticity/current status has not been confirmed. | No |
| Pending Approval | Credential has been verified but public use has not been approved. | No |
| Expired | Credential was valid previously but is no longer current. | No, unless historical use is explicitly approved |
| Suspended | Credential is currently suspended. | No |
| Revoked | Credential has been withdrawn. | No |
| Restricted | Valid credential but not approved for public disclosure. | No |
| Archived | Historical record retained internally. | No |
| Unknown | Credential status cannot currently be established. | No |

13. Certification vs Registration

Do not confuse:

Registration
Certification
License
Accreditation
Membership

These are different types of credentials.

A commercial registration does not automatically establish that a company is "certified."

A membership does not automatically establish accreditation.

A license does not automatically establish quality certification.

25. Expired Credentials

Expired credentials must not remain presented as current.

When a credential expires:

update its status;
remove or revise public references;
update claims supported by the credential;
remove obsolete badges/logos;
preserve historical records where appropriate.

27. Credential Conflicts

Do not merge records.

→ Resolve
→ Update Record
→ Revalidate Public Claim

Evidence is more valuable than badge quantity.

Final Rule

A credential is evidence only when its holder, issuer, scope, validity, authenticity, and permitted use are known.

A credential belonging to someone in Baghlaf's supply network is not automatically a credential belonging to Baghlaf Steel.
