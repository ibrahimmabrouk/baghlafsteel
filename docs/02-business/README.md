# Business Source of Truth

The `02-business` folder contains the verified and management-approved business information used by the Baghlaf Steel website and project.

These documents define **what is factually known and approved about the business**. They are not strategy documents, marketing drafts, or AI-generated assumptions.

## Files

### [`company-facts.md`](./company-facts.md)

Defines the verified identity and factual profile of Baghlaf Steel.

Answers:

**Who is Baghlaf Steel?**

---

### [`approved-contacts.md`](./approved-contacts.md)

Contains verified and approved official contact channels and representatives.

Answers:

**How can buyers officially contact or verify Baghlaf Steel?**

---

### [`approved-products.md`](./approved-products.md)

Contains the products that Baghlaf Steel has verified and approved for representation.

Answers:

**What products are approved?**

---

### [`approved-supply-sources.md`](./approved-supply-sources.md)

Contains verified supply sources and the approved relationship between Baghlaf Steel and each source.

Answers:

**Through which approved sources and relationships can Baghlaf obtain or coordinate supply?**

---

### [`credentials.md`](./credentials.md)

Contains verified credentials, registrations, certifications, licenses, memberships, and other formal evidence, including who holds each credential.

Answers:

**What formal evidence exists, and who does it belong to?**

---

### [`claims-matrix.md`](./claims-matrix.md)

Controls what may be communicated publicly about Baghlaf Steel, its products, supply sources, relationships, credentials, capabilities, and other business matters.

Answers:

**What are we allowed to claim publicly?**

---

### [`terminology.md`](./terminology.md)

Defines the controlled English and Arabic terminology that should be used consistently across the project.

Answers:

**What words should we use to describe the business accurately?**

---
