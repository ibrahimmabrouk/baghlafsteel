# Terminology

## Purpose

This document defines the controlled terminology for Baghlaf Steel across the website, business documentation, product catalog, requirement flow, Arabic and English content, metadata, structured data, AI-generated content, and internal project communication.

Its purpose is to ensure that important business concepts are represented consistently and accurately.

Terminology must reflect the actual Baghlaf Steel business model.

A word must not be selected merely because it:

- sounds more professional;
- is common in the industry;
- is preferred by an AI model;
- improves SEO;
- appears on another company's website;
- sounds stronger commercially.

Where a term has a specific business meaning, that meaning must be preserved.

---

# 1. Terminology Status

Each controlled term may have one of the following statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Approved | Officially approved for the specified use. |
| Preferred | Recommended term when no stronger business restriction applies. |
| Conditional | Allowed only in the specified context. |
| Restricted | Should not be used without explicit approval. |
| Prohibited | Must not be used for Baghlaf Steel. |
| Pending Approval | Candidate term awaiting management confirmation. |
| Pending Verification | Meaning or usage has not yet been verified. |

The status applies to the term and its defined usage, not merely to the word itself.

55. Terminology in AI-Generated Content

AI-generated content must pass terminology control before publication.

The agent should ask:

Is this the approved term?
Does the term accurately describe the entity?
Does the term preserve the intended business meaning?
Is the term allowed publicly?
Does the Arabic wording preserve the same meaning?

A grammatically excellent sentence can still be commercially incorrect because of one wrong term.

A terminology change may have wider impact than changing a single sentence.
