# Approved Products

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative business source of truth for the products that Baghlaf Steel has approved for representation, supply, and/or publication through the website.

Only products explicitly verified and approved by Baghlaf Steel management may be treated as approved business information.

This document must distinguish between:

- products Baghlaf is approved to represent;
- products Baghlaf is currently able to source;
- products currently available;
- products confirmed for a specific buyer requirement;
- products that are no longer approved for public presentation.

The presence of a product in this document does not automatically mean that the product is currently available, allocated, or guaranteed for supply.

---

# 1. Product Approval Status

Each product should have an explicit status.

Use only the statuses appropriate to the actual business process:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending Verification | Product information has been received but has not yet been verified. |
| Pending Approval | Product information has been verified but is awaiting management approval. |
| Approved | Product is approved for the specified business/public use. |
| Under Review | Existing approval is being reviewed. |
| Inactive | Product is no longer approved for active representation. |
| Archived | Historical record retained for reference but not active. |

Only products marked **Approved** should be treated as approved public product information.

25. Product Change Management

When product information changes:

verify the new information;
update the source-of-truth record;
confirm management approval where required;
update affected website content;
update Arabic content;
update structured data where applicable;
update product documents;
validate related supply-source information;
test the production result.
26. Product Removal

A product should not be silently removed from project memory when it becomes inactive.

Where relevant, preserve:

product identity;
previous status;
reason for deactivation;
date;
replacement product if applicable.

An inactive product should no longer appear publicly unless there is an approved historical reason.

33. AI Rules

AI agents must:

treat this document as a business source of truth;
never invent products;
never invent product specifications;
never infer grades;
never infer standards;
never infer supply relationships;
never assume current availability;
never assume inventory;
never convert capability into guarantee;
never convert examples into official quantities;
never create unsupported product claims;
never identify a manufacturer relationship without approval;
never publish pending or unknown information as fact;
preserve the distinction between public and internal data;
flag missing or conflicting information.

When required product information is missing, the correct response is:

Unknown / Pending Verification

not an AI-generated replacement.

34. Website Publication Rule

A product should only move from business source of truth to public website when the required publication information is approved.

Conceptually:

Product Data
