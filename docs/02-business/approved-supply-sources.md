# Approved Supply Sources

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative business source of truth for external companies, manufacturers, producers, suppliers, partners, distributors, agents, or other supply sources that Baghlaf Steel has verified and approved for association with its business and/or public website.

The purpose of this document is to ensure that the website accurately represents:

- who the supply source is;
- what type of entity it is;
- what relationship Baghlaf has with it;
- which products are associated with it;
- whether the relationship may be publicly disclosed;
- whether its name or logo may be displayed;
- what claims may and may not be made about the relationship.

This document does **not** imply that every approved source is:

- a current supplier;
- currently available;
- exclusively connected to Baghlaf;
- authorized for every product;
- authorized for every market;
- approved for public disclosure.

Each of those conditions must be explicitly verified.

# 1. Core Terminology

The following terms must remain distinct:

```text
Supply Source
Manufacturer
Producer
Supplier
Partner
Distributor
Agent
Authorized Distributor

A Supply Source is a broad project term describing an external source from which Baghlaf may obtain or coordinate supply.

A Manufacturer / Producer is an entity that physically produces the relevant steel product.

A Supplier is an entity from which Baghlaf obtains or sources products under an approved commercial relationship.

A Partner, Distributor, or Agent represents a different relationship and must only be used where explicitly approved.

These terms must never be substituted automatically.

2. Approval Status

Each supply source must have an explicit status.

Status	Meaning
Pending Verification	Information exists but the source or relationship has not been verified.
Pending Approval	The source and/or relationship has been verified but is not yet approved for the intended use.
Approved	The source and relationship are approved for the specified use.
Under Review	Existing approval is being reviewed.
Inactive	The source or relationship is no longer active/approved.
Archived	Historical information retained for reference but not active.

Only Approved records may be treated as active business truth.

22. Public vs Internal Relationship

A source can be commercially important while remaining private.

Each relationship should therefore specify:

Internal
Public
Restricted
Internal

May be used internally but not disclosed publicly.

Public

May be referenced publicly within approved wording.

Restricted

Only specifically authorized personnel may access or use the information.

23. Supply Network Publication

The website may eventually present approved supply-network information through:

source listings;
product relationships;
manufacturer references;
supply-network explanations;
approved logos;
supporting documentation.

However, public presentation must never make the website appear to be:

a marketplace;
an open supplier directory;
a public database of every manufacturer;
an inventory system.

Only approved information should be exposed.

24. Source Confidentiality

27. Change Management

When a supply-source relationship changes:

verify the change;
update this source of truth;
update relationship status;
update affected products;
review public disclosure permissions;
update website content;
update logos/assets where applicable;
update Arabic content;
validate production.

Do not silently remove historical relationship information where it may be relevant to project memory.

28. Relationship Termination

When a relationship ends:

Active
→ Under Review
→ Inactive

The source should be removed from public active representations where appropriate.

Historical records may remain internally.

29. Source Review

Existing approved sources should be periodically reviewed for:

identity;
relationship status;
products;
public disclosure;
logo permission;
documentation;
credentials;
supply capability.

Review frequency should be based on business volatility.

30. AI Rules

AI agents must:

treat this file as the authoritative source for approved supply-source relationships;
never invent a supplier;
never infer a relationship from a website;
never assume a manufacturer is a supplier;
never assume a supplier is a partner;
never assume an agent is an authorized distributor;
never assume exclusivity;
never infer current availability;
never infer capacity;
never infer authorization;
never publish an unapproved company logo;
never expose confidential source information;
never turn third-party information into Baghlaf business truth;
flag missing or conflicting relationships.

The correct state for unresolved information is:

Unknown
Pending Verification
Pending Approval

not an AI-generated conclusion.

Final Rule

A company is not an approved Baghlaf supply source because it manufactures the right product, appears in a search result, or has been mentioned by an intermediary.

It becomes an approved supply source only when its identity, relationship, scope, and permitted use have been verified and approved.
