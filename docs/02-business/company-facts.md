30. Fact vs Claim

A factual record and a marketing claim are not always identical.

Example:

Fact:
Baghlaf is based in Saudi Arabia.

Claim:
"Your trusted gateway to Saudi steel."


The first is a factual identity statement.

The second is positioning/marketing language and belongs in the claims/content systems.

32. Fact Conflict Protocol

When two records disagree:

Source A
   ↕
Conflict
   ↕
Source B

The agent must:

1. Identify the conflicting facts.
2. Identify their sources.
3. Determine which source has appropriate authority.
4. Avoid publishing either conflicting claim until resolved.
5. Record the approved result.

The agent must not silently choose whichever version appears newer, more professional, or more complete.
