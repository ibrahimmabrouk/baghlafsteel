# Approved Contacts

## Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for Baghlaf Steel's approved public and operational contact information.

Only contact information that has been explicitly verified and approved may be treated as official within the website, public communications, metadata, structured data, forms, email templates, or other project outputs.

AI agents and developers must not invent, infer, reconstruct, or "clean up" contact information that is not explicitly approved here.

---

# 1. Authority & Status

This document is part of the Baghlaf Steel business source of truth.

Information in this file should be treated as:

- **Approved** — verified and approved for the specified use.
- **Pending Verification** — information exists but has not yet been verified.
- **Pending Approval** — information has been verified but has not yet been approved for the specified public use.
- **Inactive** — previously approved but no longer valid.
- **Unknown** — information has not been provided or confirmed.

Only **Approved** information may be published publicly unless a separate approval explicitly permits otherwise.

15. Public vs Internal Contacts

Not every operational contact should be published.

Each contact should be classified as:

Public
Internal
Restricted
Public

May appear on the public website.

Internal

Used by employees/developers and should not appear publicly.

Restricted

May be accessible only to specifically authorized personnel.

Sensitive operational contacts should not be exposed simply because they exist.

16. Contact Verification Requirements

17. Change Management

When an approved contact changes:

update this source of truth;
record the previous status appropriately;
update affected website locations;
update email templates;
update verification information;
validate English and Arabic;
verify production after release.

Do not silently replace historical information without understanding whether the old contact must remain documented.

19. AI Rules

AI agents must:

use this document as the authoritative business source for approved contacts;
never invent missing contact information;
never infer an official contact from a third-party source;
never treat a domain as official without approval;
never publish an unapproved phone number;
never create a fictional department email;
never assume a person's job title;
never expose internal/restricted contacts publicly;
flag conflicting contact information;
preserve Pending Verification, Pending Approval, and Unknown states.

If a requested page requires a missing contact, the correct behavior is to flag the missing information rather than fabricate a replacement.

### One important recommendation

I would **not fill this file from Google, LinkedIn, old Baghlaf websites, business directories, or search results yet**.

For this project, contact information is part of the **trust architecture**, so the cleanest workflow is:

```text
Management
   ↓
Confirm / Approve
   ↓
approved-contacts.md
   ↓
Website
   ↓
Verification System

That gives you a very strong audit trail and prevents the exact problem you're trying to solve—buyers finding multiple "official" Baghlaf identities online—from being reproduced inside the new website.
