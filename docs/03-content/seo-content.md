# SEO Content

## Purpose

This document defines the SEO content strategy and controlled SEO information for the Baghlaf Steel website.

It covers:

- search-intent alignment;
- page-level SEO messaging;
- keywords and topics;
- page titles;
- meta descriptions;
- headings;
- URL slugs;
- internal-linking language;
- structured-data content;
- English SEO content;
- Arabic SEO content;
- SEO content status;
- search-oriented content opportunities.

SEO exists to make the approved Baghlaf Steel business information easier for relevant buyers to discover and understand.

SEO must never be used to:

- invent business claims;
- exaggerate company capabilities;
- imply manufacturing;
- imply unsupported supplier relationships;
- create false geographic coverage;
- manufacture customer statistics;
- create fake credentials;
- turn capability into availability;
- weaken controlled terminology.

5. Search Intent

SEO content should account for different search intents.

Informational

The user wants to understand something.

Examples:

steel grades
steel standards
steel product specifications

Only topics Baghlaf is qualified and approved to discuss should be developed.

Commercial Investigation

The user is comparing suppliers, products, or supply options.

Examples:

steel supplier Saudi Arabia
steel trading company Saudi Arabia
steel sourcing company Saudi Arabia

Such terms require care because they can imply business classifications or capabilities.

Transactional

The user is ready to engage commercially.

Examples:

buy steel
steel quotation
steel requirement
steel supplier contact

The website should route relevant visitors toward the appropriate approved commercial action.

Navigational

The user is trying to find Baghlaf specifically.

Examples may include searches around:

Baghlaf Steel
Baghlaf Saudi Arabia
Baghlaf steel contact
Baghlaf official website

These searches are particularly important to the project's trust objective.

11. Product Capability vs Availability in SEO

SEO copy must preserve:

Product Capability
≠
Current Availability

For example:

"Steel products we can source"

is conceptually different from:

"Steel currently in stock"

Do not target or create "in stock" language unless actual inventory exists and is approved.

24. Content Depth

Do not use arbitrary word-count targets.

A useful product page with 500 accurate words is better than a 2,000-word page filled with repetition.

36. Image SEO

Images should have meaningful:

alt text where appropriate;

Alt text should describe the actual image.

It should not be used as keyword stuffing.

For example, do not label a generic industrial image:

"Best steel supplier in Saudi Arabia"

unless that description actually represents the image and approved context.
